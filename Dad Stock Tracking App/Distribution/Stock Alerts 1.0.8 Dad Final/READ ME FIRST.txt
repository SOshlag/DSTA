STOCK ALERTS FOR macOS
======================

SYSTEM REQUIREMENT
macOS 13 Ventura or newer. This app supports both Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

INSTALL
1. Double-click the ZIP file to open it.
2. Drag Stock Alerts.app into the Mac's Applications folder.
3. In Applications, Control-click Stock Alerts and choose Open.
4. If macOS asks for confirmation, choose Open again.
5. Stock Alerts opens its main window and also appears in the Dock.
6. It also provides a chart-line icon in the menu bar when macOS has room.
7. If the menu-bar icon is hidden, click Stock Alerts in the Dock instead.

CONNECT FREE MARKET DATA
1. Create your own Alpaca account at https://alpaca.markets/.
2. Open a free Paper Trading account and generate API keys.
3. In Stock Alerts, open the main window and click Market Data.
4. Choose IEX Real-Time Trades.
5. Paste the API Key ID and Secret Key, then click Save & Connect.

The credentials are saved only in this Mac's encrypted Keychain. They are not
included in the app or this ZIP. Never send the Secret Key to another person.

CREATE AN ALERT
1. Click New Price Alert.
2. Type a ticker or company name and select a suggested U.S. stock or ETF.
3. Choose one of four alert directions:
   - Buy Below: last trade is at or below the target.
   - Buy Above: last trade is at or above the target.
   - Sell Below: last trade is at or below the target.
   - Sell Above: last trade is at or above the target.
4. Enter the target price, add an optional note, and click Add Alert.

SHARE YOUR TRACKING LIST
1. Open the main Stock Alerts window.
2. Click Export CSV.
3. Choose where to save the file and click Export.
4. The CSV can be opened in Numbers, Excel, or Google Sheets and shared normally.

The export contains tracked-alert details only. Alpaca API credentials and
other Keychain information are never included.

IMPORTANT
- Stock Alerts does not place trades or connect to brokerage orders.
- The free IEX feed covers one market-data source, so prices may differ from a
  brokerage's consolidated quote.
- SPX is an index and is not supported by the IEX stock feed. Use SPY if you
  want to monitor the S&P 500 ETF.
- The app must remain running and connected for live alerts.
- Alerts and notes stay locally on this Mac.

UNINSTALL
Quit Stock Alerts, then move Stock Alerts.app to the Trash. Saved alerts remain
in ~/Library/Application Support/DadStockAlerts unless that folder is removed.
